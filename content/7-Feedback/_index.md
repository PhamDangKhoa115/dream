> This section reflects my personal experience and opinions after completing the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) internship program.

---

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment during the internship was comfortable and focused on learning.  
Instead of being pressured by deadlines, I had the opportunity to explore technologies at my own pace while still maintaining responsibility for assigned tasks.

I also found that the environment encouraged independent thinking, which helped me improve my ability to solve problems rather than relying on step-by-step instructions.

---

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor played an important role in guiding my learning process.  
Rather than directly providing solutions, the mentor often gave hints and directions, allowing me to figure things out on my own. This approach helped me build stronger problem-solving skills.

The admin team also ensured that all necessary materials and instructions were available, making it easier to follow the program.

---

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship content was closely related to my major, especially in web development and system design.  
However, it also introduced me to cloud technologies such as AWS, which I had not deeply explored before.

This combination of familiar and new knowledge helped me expand my understanding of real-world systems.

---

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Throughout the internship, I developed both technical and practical skills, including:

- Building frontend interfaces using React
- Working with APIs and handling asynchronous operations
- Understanding how cloud services like S3, Cognito, and API Gateway work together
- Debugging real issues such as CORS errors and authentication problems
- Thinking more clearly about system structure and data flow

These experiences were very valuable because they reflect real-world development scenarios.

---

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The overall atmosphere was supportive and respectful.  
Even though I was an intern, I felt comfortable sharing ideas and asking questions.

There was no strict hierarchy in communication, which made collaboration easier and more effective.

---

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provided flexibility in learning and working time, which helped me balance my schedule effectively.  
Additionally, the opportunity to work on a real project and apply knowledge in practice was the most valuable benefit.

---

### Additional Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  The most satisfying experience was building a working system from start to finish.  
  I was able to create a frontend application that interacts with cloud services and handles real user actions such as uploading files and displaying processed results.

This gave me a clearer understanding of how different parts of a system connect together.

---

- **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  One improvement could be providing a clearer overview of the learning path at the beginning.  
  Having a structured roadmap would help interns understand what they are expected to achieve in each stage of the program.

---

- **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  Yes, I would recommend this program.  
  It is a good opportunity to gain hands-on experience with modern technologies, especially for students who want to learn about cloud computing and real-world development.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  It would be beneficial to have more sessions focused on real-world scenarios, such as system debugging, performance optimization, or deployment strategies.

This would help interns better understand how systems operate in production environments.

---

- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  Yes, I would like to continue learning in this program if possible.  
  I believe there are still many advanced topics that I can explore further.

---

- **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  Overall, this internship was a meaningful experience for me.  
  It helped me transition from learning theory to applying knowledge in practice.

I feel more confident in my ability to work with cloud technologies and build real-world applications after completing the program.
